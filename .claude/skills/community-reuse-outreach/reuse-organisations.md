# UK reuse organisations directory

Verified UK reuse organisations and networks (73 entries), grouped by material type, for the Tier-1 harvest in `SKILL.md`. Each entry says how to find the LOCAL branch/member for a given town. Compiled 2026-06 from verified web research; re-check URLs periodically.

**How to use:** for a clearance, take the donor's item clusters, pick the matching sections below, and use each org's *find-local* route (postcode search / members map / directory) to get the organisations actually operating in the target geography. National gifting networks (Freegle, Freecycle, Olio) and the Reuse Network member finder are the fastest routes to local takers.


## General reuse networks & finders

- **[Freegle](https://www.ilovefreegle.org)** (national network) - takes Any reusable items - furniture, household goods, clothing, electronics, garden items, and more, all given away free. *Find local:* Enter your town or postcode on the homepage (ilovefreegle.org) to browse your local Freegle community's listings.
- **[Freecycle](https://www.freecycle.org)** (national network) - takes Any reusable items given away free - no specific item restrictions stated; volunteer-moderated, no scams or adult content. *Find local:* Use the 'Find A Town' search on freecycle.org or browse all towns at freecycle.org/find-towns.
- **[Trash Nothing](https://trashnothing.com)** (national network) - takes Any free reusable items - books, clothes, furniture, games, electronics, appliances, tools, building materials. *Find local:* Browse UK groups by region at trashnothing.com/groups/united-kingdom/ then select region to find local groups; also available via app.
- **[Reuse Network](https://reuse-network.org.uk)** (national network) - takes Furniture (sofas, beds, tables, chairs), household goods, electrical appliances, kitchenware - must be clean and in working/usable condition; upholstered furniture needs fire label. *Find local:* Postcode or town search via the 'Find items' or 'Donate items' pages at reuse-network.org.uk - filter by item type and distance to show nearest member charities with contact details.
- **[Olio](https://olioapp.com)** (national network) - takes Food (surplus from shops and households), household items, clothes, books, toys, toiletries - anything free to give neighbours. *Find local:* Download the Olio app; location is set automatically to show nearby listings and local Food Waste Hero collections from retailers.
- **[Recycle Now (WRAP) - Recycling Locator](https://www.recyclenow.com/recycling-locator)** (national) - takes Wide range of materials and items including plastics, clothing, electricals, batteries, building waste, paint - covers items not accepted in home kerbside collection. *Find local:* Enter postcode into the locator at recyclenow.com/recycling-locator to find local authority kerbside collection details and nearby drop-off points for specific items.
- **[Recycle Your Electricals](https://recycleyourelectricals.org.uk)** (national) - takes Anything with a plug, battery or cable - phones, laptops, TVs, cables, batteries, lightbulbs, smart devices, vapes, toys, household appliances. *Find local:* Enter postcode at recycleyourelectricals.org.uk/electrical-recycling-near-me/ to find nearest of ~30,000 drop-off points (recycling centres, shops, charities, community hubs); also shows 250+ repair locations.
- **[Library of Things](https://www.libraryofthings.co.uk)** (local finder) - takes Tools, DIY equipment, gardening equipment, camping/outdoor gear, party supplies and other high-quality items for borrowing (rental model, not gifting). *Find local:* Use 'Find your nearest location' on the homepage; currently 22 locations mostly in London. Community organisations and councils can license their own via participate.libraryofthings.co.uk.
- **[Ethical Consumer - Libraries of Things Directory](https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/home-garden/library-things-directory)** (directory) - takes Directory covering all types of sharing libraries/libraries of things across the UK - tools, equipment, household items, outdoor gear etc. *Find local:* Interactive map and A-Z directory of 100+ libraries of things across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland; organised by country/region.
- **[Repair Cafe (Repair Café International Foundation)](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/visit/)** (national network) - takes Broken household items for free repair by volunteers - clothing, furniture, electrical appliances, bicycles, crockery, toys. *Find local:* Search for a local Repair Cafe using the 'Visit' map/directory at repaircafe.org/en/visit/ - filter by country (UK) and region.
- **[The Restart Project](https://therestartproject.org)** (national network) - takes Broken electronics for free repair - laptops, smartphones, tablets, Kindles, audio equipment, toasters and other small electricals. *Find local:* Browse upcoming Restart Party events filtered by United Kingdom at therestartproject.org/parties/ - filter by country and region.
- **[Community RePaint](https://communityrepaint.org.uk)** (national network) - takes Leftover usable paint (household, commercial, trade) - redistributes part and full containers of paint at low cost; accepts 60+ litre quantities for remanufacture. *Find local:* Use 'Find Schemes' on the homepage at communityrepaint.org.uk to locate nearest collection and distribution point.
- **[UK Men's Sheds Association (UKMSA)](https://www.menssheds.org.uk)** (national network) - takes Community workshop spaces where members repair, make and repurpose items - tools, timber, metals, general materials for projects. *Find local:* Search the shed directory at menssheds.org.uk/sheds/ by location to find the nearest Men's Shed.
- **[Reuseful UK - Scrapstores Directory](https://www.reusefuluk.org/directory)** (directory) - takes Scrap and waste materials from businesses for reuse in arts, crafts and play - offcuts, fabric, foam, card, packaging, plastic shapes, wood, yarn and similar creative materials. *Find local:* Search directory at reusefuluk.org/directory by scrapstore name, city/town or region; 65 registered members across England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Republic of Ireland.

## Furniture & white goods reuse

Phone: 0800 085 8339.
- **[British Heart Foundation Furniture & Electrical](https://www.bhf.org.uk/shop/donating-furniture)** (national) - takes Furniture (sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining sets etc.), electricals and some white goods (fridges, washing machines, dishwashers - call ahead); excludes washers/dryers of certain models, gas appliances, items without fire safety labels. *Find local:* Postcode search on site to check collection availability; store finder filtered to furniture & electrical shops at bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/find-bhf-near-you?servicetype=furniture-electrical-store.
- **[Emmaus UK](https://emmaus.org.uk/donate-goods/)** (national network) - takes Furniture (sofas, wardrobes, dining sets, beds, chests of drawers), white goods (fridges, freezers etc.), electricals, clothing, homewares, bric-a-brac. Soft furnishings and electricals require fire safety labels. Accepted items vary by community. *Find local:* Region and community selector on the donate-goods page; browse 40+ named communities or filter by region (East of England, Greater London, Midlands, North East, North West, Scotland, South East, South West, Wales, Yorkshire). Phone: 0300 303 7555.
- **[British Red Cross Furniture & Electrical Charity Shops](https://www.redcross.org.uk/shop/furniture-and-electrical-charity-shops)** (national) - takes Furniture (sofas, beds, desks, dining tables, armchairs), electricals (TVs, DVD players, small appliances), some white goods (fridges, washing machines, dishwashers - call ahead). Excludes duvets, pillows, mattresses, gas appliances, microwaves. *Find local:* Shop finder on site (redcross.org.uk/shop) with filter for 'Furniture and Electrical' shop type; some shops offer home collection - contact local shop to check.
- **[Sue Ryder Charity Shops (furniture & white goods)](https://www.sueryder.org/shop-with-us/find-a-shop/)** (national) - takes Furniture (sofas, tables, beds, wardrobes), small electricals; some superstores also stock reconditioned white goods. Does NOT collect white goods (fridges, washing machines) via the free collection service - furniture and small electricals only for collection. *Find local:* Postcode search on the shop finder at sueryder.org/shop-with-us/find-a-shop/ with filters for shop type (furniture, white goods, electrical, etc.). Free furniture collection service bookable by postcode at sueryder.org/shop-with-us/donate-to-our-shops/free-furniture-collection-service/.
- **[Salvation Army Trading - Donation Centres](https://salvationarmytrading.org.uk/)** (national) - takes Furniture (beds, sofas, wardrobes, mattresses, curtains), white goods and larger electrical items (PAT tested on site), clothing, homewares. Excludes gas appliances, electric showers, hardwired items, IT equipment with hard drives. *Find local:* Charity shop, donation centre and clothing bank locator at salvationarmytrading.org.uk/find-us/ - also accessible via salvationarmy.org.uk/map-page. Donation centres (20+ UK-wide) accept furniture and white goods; standard charity shops generally do not.
- **[Furniture Donation Network](https://furnituredonationnetwork.org/)** (national) - takes Furniture in excellent/very good/good condition (wardrobes, desks, sofas, armchairs etc.) and working electrical appliances including white goods (cookers, fridges, freezers, washing machines). Upholstered items must have fire safety labels. *Find local:* Online booking at furnituredonationnetwork.org/bookings/ - upload photo, enter postcode and contact details; a local partner charity arranges collection. Operated by Recycling Lives charity (no. 1116562).
- **[Oxfam Furniture Shops](https://www.oxfam.org.uk/about-us/faq/oxfam-shops-uk/furniture-shops/)** (directory) - takes Second-hand furniture and homeware. Does NOT accept large white goods, gas appliances, microwaves or electric heaters. Limited number of specialist furniture shops only. *Find local:* Only six specialist furniture shops in the UK (Huddersfield, Liverpool, Mirfield, Rochester, Sheffield, Southampton). Shop finder at oxfam.org.uk/shops/ but furniture shops are few and fixed - check the list on the furniture-shops page first.

## IT / computers / devices

- **[Computer Aid International](https://www.computeraid.org/)** (national) - takes Laptops, desktop PCs, monitors, mobile phones, tablets, servers, switches, printers, storage devices, networking gear, cables, keyboards, mice. Takes bulk office IT - pallet-based collection with data destruction certificates. *Find local:* National UK-wide collection service; contact directly to arrange pallet collection from anywhere in mainland UK. Drop-off accepted at their Manchester facility. No branch finder needed - single national operation.
- **[The Turing Trust](https://turingtrust.co.uk/)** (national) - takes Laptops and desktop PCs (must run Windows 10 or macOS 12 Monterey or newer, under ~10 years old), monitors, RAM, hard drives, cables, keyboards, mice, headphones, projectors, network switches (8-80 port). Takes bulk office IT - free UK-wide collection for large volumes (20+ PCs); free data destruction for 20+ PCs. *Find local:* National UK-wide free collection for large donations. Single facility in Edinburgh (Pentland Industrial Estate, Loanhead EH20 9QH) for drop-off by appointment Mon-Fri 10am-4:30pm. Book via the Give Computers form on the website.
- **[Computers 4 Good (formerly Computers 4 Charity)](https://computers4good.com/)** (national) - takes Laptops and desktop PCs (Intel 5th-gen i-series or newer, under ~10 years old) and associated IT hardware. Takes bulk business donations - free collection for 10+ computers. *Find local:* Contact to arrange free collection for 10+ devices (03000 11 22 33 or contact-us@computers4good.com). Also runs regional Tech 4 Reuse drop-in events across the UK - check website for upcoming dates and locations.
- **[Business2Schools](https://business2schools.org/)** (national) - takes Laptops, desktop PCs, and other office technology donated by businesses, redistributed to UK state schools. Suited to bulk office IT clearances. *Find local:* Businesses contact the charity directly (info@business2schools.com) to arrange collection or donation. No public drop-off finder - they match surplus business IT to UK schools with greatest need.
- **[Good Things Foundation - National Device Bank](https://www.goodthingsfoundation.org/our-services/national-device-bank)** (national network) - takes Laptops, tablets, and mobile phones. Accepts bulk corporate and public-sector donations via exclusive tech partner Reconome (full ITAD process). *Find local:* Donate via Reconome (recono.me) for corporate/bulk donations. To find a local Digital Inclusion Hub receiving devices, use the map at https://www.goodthingsfoundation.org/find-support/map - over 8,000 member organisations across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
- **[Community Calling (Hubbub / Virgin Media O2)](https://hubbub.org.uk/community-calling)** (national) - takes Smartphones only (no cracked screens or backs, must hold charge, not MDM or network locked). Individuals and businesses (5+ devices). *Find local:* Individuals sign up at hubbub.org.uk/community-calling for a freepost return envelope. Businesses with 5+ phones email communitycalling@hubbub.org.uk for collection. Hubbub distributes via community groups and councils - donors do not select recipients.
- **[The Restart Project - UK computer donation directory](https://therestartproject.org/where-to-donate-your-computer/)** (directory) - takes Laptops, computers, tablets, and other electronics. Operates a searchable directory of UK local organisations that accept computer donations for reuse and repair. *Find local:* Search the directory at therestartproject.org/where-to-donate-your-computer/ by location and item type to find local reuse projects across the UK accepting individual donations.
- **[Catbytes](https://catbytes.community/donate-your-device)** (regional) - takes Laptops, tablets, and smartphones for refurbishment and redistribution to digitally excluded individuals in the community. *Find local:* Drop-off at five locations in the London Borough of Lewisham: Crofton Park Library (main office), Deptford Library, Downham Library, Telegraph Hill Centre, Sydenham Library. Contact info@catbytes.community or 0208 314 4702.

Over 100 member schemes from the Isle of Wight to the Scottish Highlands.
- **[Community Wood Recycling](https://www.communitywoodrecycling.org.uk)** (national network) - takes Waste timber of all kinds collected from businesses; pallet wood; reclaimed timber sold on from their wood stores. *Find local:* Postcode or city search at communitywoodrecycling.org.uk/wood-stores/ with distance filter (10-500 miles). 24 social enterprises across the UK.
- **[SalvoWEB](https://www.salvoweb.com)** (directory) - takes Reclaimed and architectural salvage building materials: bricks, roof tiles and slates, flooring, fireplaces, doors, windows, sanitaryware, radiators, lighting, garden items and more. *Find local:* Directory search by location, region, speciality or item type at salvoweb.com - lists hundreds of UK reclamation yards and architectural salvage dealers. Also accessible via the futuREuse sub-directory at salvoweb.com/salvo-directory/fcrbe.
- **[futuREuse (by Salvo)](https://futureuse.co.uk)** (directory) - takes Reclaimed building elements and construction materials: bricks, tiles, slates, flooring, structural timber, joinery, sanitaryware, radiators and architectural salvage. *Find local:* Directory search by location/region and material category at futureuse.co.uk/futureuse-directory/ - covers 500 verified businesses across UK and Ireland.
- **[Reuseful UK (Scrapstore Network)](https://www.reusefuluk.org)** (national network) - takes Clean reusable scrap materials from businesses: offcuts, packaging, craft materials, textiles, paper, card, foam and similar items suitable for creative reuse by children and community groups. *Find local:* Directory of 65 member scrapstores searchable by scrapstore name, town/city or region at reusefuluk.org/directory. Contact hello@scrapstuff.co.uk if the directory does not load.
- **[Children's Scrapstore](https://www.childrensscrapstore.co.uk)** (local finder) - takes Clean industrial and commercial scrap: offcuts, packaging, textiles, foam, paper, card, tubes, reels and other materials suitable for children's art, craft and play. *Find local:* Single Bristol-based organisation (St Werburghs, BS2 9LB); the Bristol Reuse Directory on their site lists other local reuse outlets. For other UK locations, use the Reuseful UK directory at reusefuluk.org/directory.
