# Reference: categories, clusters, gates

## The 12 search categories (one research agent each)

1. Homelessness, housing, resettlement, refuges, care leavers
2. Refugee, asylum, migrant support
3. Community centres and halls
4. Faith communities running community programmes
5. Food banks, pantries, community kitchens, cafes
6. Mental health peer support and day centres
7. Disability, sensory, learning disability, neurodivergence
8. Youth organisations (scouts, guides, cadets, youth clubs)
9. Older-people services
10. Furniture reuse, Men's Sheds, repair (**Tier 1**)
11. Schools, nurseries, PTAs
12. Small charities, CICs, maker spaces

## Item cluster -> typical organisational fit

| Cluster | Item examples | Typical fit |
|---|---|---|
| Office setup | Desks, under-desk drawers, cabinets | Small charities/CICs moving or expanding, refugee support offices, disability advice centres |
| Hall seating | Stackable chairs (metal/plastic/wooden) | Churches, community halls, scout huts, schools, sports clubs |
| Waiting / lounge | Armchairs, sofa, low table | Care homes, day centres, drop-ins, GP waiting rooms, refugee drop-ins |
| Events tables | Trestle/circle/corner tables | Markets, fetes, community meals, scout groups |
| Storage | Lockable cabinets, cupboards, bookcases | Schools, youth clubs, tool libraries, book swaps |
| Kitchen | Fridge, white goods | Food banks, community kitchens, pantries, community cafes |
| Bulk consumables | Envelopes, stationery in quantity | Mail-fundraising charities, parish offices, membership orgs |
| Fixtures | Wall clocks, step ladders | Any community building furnishing a space |

Cluster the donor's actual items into the smallest set of these that covers the list. Each Tier-2 outreach email offers exactly ONE cluster.

## Hard-gates reference card

An organisation enters the shortlist **if and only if**:
1. **Activity** - one specific dated URL within the last 12 months showing operations in the target geography.
2. **Email** - a published address (website or social "about"), not a contact form, not phone-only.
3. **Reality** - genuine operations in the target geography, not a registered address for an entity operating elsewhere.

**Can provide activity evidence:** Google Search hits; the org's own website with a dated page/post; dated Facebook / Instagram / X / LinkedIn posts; local news (e.g. the area's local paper / online news); local CVS / Community-Works event listings; dated job ads.

**CANNOT provide activity evidence (only confirm legal status once otherwise found):** Charity Commission register, Companies House, CIC regulator, old directories, GOV.UK "Get Information About Schools".

## Scoring (four 1-3 axes, total 4-12)

| Axis | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit** (cluster serves mission) | stated service need | general infrastructure fit | tangential |
| **Capacity** (collect + store this scale) | own premises + transport | premises only | neither |
| **Recency** (activity signal) | within last month | within 6 months | within 12 months |
| **Reachability** (email quality) | dedicated contact role | generic inbox (info@/office@) | social-only contact |

Tier-1 candidates bypass Fit (fit is assumed). The scoring model must cite the evidence for each score; a human can override.

## Tier-1 sources (exhaustive, structured)

Reuse Network member directory; UK Men's Sheds Association map (cross-check activity - some are dormant); Library of Things / Share UK / repaircafe.org; local reuse operators; **Freegle internal** previous bulk takers in the area (`messages_outcomes` + `chat_messages` where a replier collected >= N items in one chat); any operator's private network; response history from prior clearances (auto-promote to Tier 1 after ~2 successful collections in 18 months).
