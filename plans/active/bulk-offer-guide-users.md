# Clearance offers: a guide for Freeglers

A **clearance offer** lets you give away lots of things in one go. Instead of posting each item
separately, you make a single post that holds a whole list of items. Each item has its own name,
quantity, condition, size and photo. People who see your post can browse the list and tick exactly
the items they want, and say how many. You then decide who gets what.

This is useful for a house clearance, an office move, a school clearout, or any time you have a pile
of things to rehome at once.

There are two sides to a clearance offer:

- **The giver** (you, if you are offering the items) - you build the list, then manage who gets what.
- **The replier** (someone who wants some of your items) - they pick the items they want and arrange
  to collect.

---

## For the giver: creating your offer

### Building your list of items

When you start a clearance offer you choose one of two ways to enter your items:

1. **Type them in.** You get a simple table. For each item you fill in:
   - **Item name** (for example "Office chair")
   - **Quantity** - how many you have
   - **Condition** - choose from New, Like new, Good, Used, Poor, or Not sure
   - **Size** - free text (for example "60x60x90cm"), optional

   Click **Add another item** for each new row. A running total of items shows below the table.

2. **Upload a spreadsheet.** If you already have a list, download the template (an `.xlsx` file),
   fill in one row per item, and upload it. Freegle reads `.xlsx`, `.csv` and `.tsv` files, works
   out the columns automatically (name, quantity, condition, size, photo link, description), and
   drops everything into the table so you can check and tidy it before posting. You can mix the two:
   upload a spreadsheet, then edit rows by hand.

You can include up to **200 items** in one clearance.

### Adding photos

There are three ways to put a photo on an item:

- **Per item:** tap the camera icon on a row and pick a photo from your device. It uploads straight
  away and shows as a thumbnail on that row.
- **Batch tray:** upload a pile of photos at once. They appear in a strip, and you drag (or tap)
  each one onto the item it belongs to.
- **Photo link in your spreadsheet:** put a web address (starting with `http://` or `https://`) in
  the photo column of your spreadsheet. The link shows as a preview straight away.

### Collection times

You can list fixed **collection windows** (for example "Saturday 10am-12pm" and "Sunday 2pm-4pm").
Repliers are then asked to tick which of your windows they can make. If you leave this blank,
repliers type their own availability in a free-text box instead.

### Access instructions (private)

You can add a private note - a gate code, where to park, which door to use, and so on. This is
**not** shown publicly. It is sent automatically to each person at the moment you allocate an item
to them, so they have what they need to come and collect.

### What happens after you post

- Each item's quantity is tracked separately, with a progress bar as items are reserved and collected.
- Your post still reads sensibly in emails and on other sites: Freegle automatically builds a tidy
  numbered summary of your catalogue (anything you wrote in the description is kept above it).
- Your post appears in members' email digests with a small table of items (photo, name, condition,
  quantity).
- New-post notifications show your title with the item count, like "Office clearance (12 items)".
- Clearance offers are not sent on to the LoveJunk marketplace, because that only handles single items.

You can edit the item list after posting. If you remove an item that someone had been promised,
their interest in it is tidied up automatically.

## For the giver: managing who gets what

Once your post is live, go to **My Posts**, find the **Your clearances** panel, and click **Manage**.
This opens the management page for that offer.

### Seeing who wants what

Under each item you see two groups of people:

- **Allocated** - people you have already reserved the item for, or who have collected it.
- **Interested** - people who have asked for the item but you have not yet decided on.

For each person you see their first name, their reputation (thumbs up/down), how many they asked for,
when they said they can collect, and a small van badge if they are already collecting other items
from the same clearance (handy for arranging one trip).

You only ever see a person's rough location (it is blurred to protect their exact address), never
their full details.

### Allocating, declining and collecting

- **Allocate** an item to a person and it moves to their "Allocated" group and the progress bar fills.
  Your access instructions are sent to them automatically at this point.
- If an item has more than one available, you can split it: allocate some to one person and some to
  another. Freegle stops you allocating more than you actually have.
- **Decline** a person for an item and they are moved to a hidden list. They get an automatic message
  letting them know the item has gone to someone else. You can **Restore** them later if things change.
- **Mark collected** when someone has picked their item up. The available count goes down accordingly.
- When every item has been fully collected, an **All items collected!** banner appears with a
  **Close this offer** button to finish the post off.

Arranging the actual collection time is done in the normal chat thread with each person - the
management page links straight to it.

## For repliers: asking for items

### What you see

When you open a clearance offer you see a catalogue of items. Each one shows a photo (if there is
one), a reference number (#1, #2 and so on), the name, how many are available, a condition badge and
the size.

### Saying what you want

- Flip the **I'd like this** switch on each item you want - pick as many as you like.
- If an item has more than one available, a **How many?** box appears so you can choose the amount.
- You can switch an item back off if you change your mind.

You can see **how many** other people are interested in each item ("3 interested - 5 req'd"), but you
**cannot** see who they are or what they each asked for. Likewise, other repliers cannot see yours.

### When you can collect

- If the giver listed fixed collection windows, tick every one that suits you. The **Register interest**
  button stays greyed out until you have ticked at least one.
- If there are no fixed windows, type your availability in the box ("Weekday evenings or Saturday
  morning").

### Registering and updating

Press **Register interest**. A single tidy message listing everything you want is sent to the giver
in a private chat. You will see a "Thanks! We've let the giver know" confirmation, and the button
changes to **Update my interest**.

You can go back any time and change which items or how many you want, then press **Update my interest**.
Your original message to the giver is updated in place - they are not spammed with duplicates.

### What happens next

- The giver decides who gets what. You get a chat notification when an item is allocated to you.
- If the giver set access instructions (address, gate code and so on), you receive them automatically
  at the same time.
- If an item goes to someone else, you get a short message letting you know.
- Once an item is allocated to you, its switch is replaced by an **Allocated to you** badge. Agree a
  collection time with the giver in the chat thread.

### Your privacy

The giver can see your first name, your rough (blurred) location, how many of each item you want, and
when you said you can collect. No other replier can see any of this.
