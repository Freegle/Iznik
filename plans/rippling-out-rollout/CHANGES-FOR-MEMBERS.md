# What's changing for members

*(This page is written for moderators, so you know what your members will experience. It
describes the member side of the **rippling out** change in plain language.)*

Rippling out changes **how posts spread to people**. Here's what an ordinary member will
notice — and what they won't.

---

## The idea, in one sentence

When someone offers something, we now show it to **people nearby first**, and then
**gradually ripple it outwards** to people further away as time passes — instead of
showing it to everyone at once.

The point is to **keep freegling local**: neighbours get first chance to collect, which
means less travel, less driving across town, and a fairer go for everyone close by. If
nobody nearby wants it, it reaches further afield soon enough.

---

## What members will see

### Posts appear in relevance order, most useful first
On the browse page, members still pick between **Nearby**, **All my communities**, and a
single community — those are unchanged in name. What's changed underneath:

- The old **travel-time slider and transport picker are gone.** Members no longer have to
  fiddle with "how far am I willing to travel" — the system now works that out for them and
  widens the area automatically over time.
- By default they see **posts that are new to them first**, then the rest — and within each
  of those groups, posts are ordered by a relevance score that weighs up how close a post
  is, how fresh it is, and how much interest it has already had, rather than simply by when
  it arrived. So a distant post that happens to be recent no longer jumps ahead of a closer
  or more relevant one. They can still switch to "Newest posted" or "Closest" (renamed from
  "Nearby") if they prefer.
- There's also a new **distance slider** in the filters, marked "Nearer" and "Further" (no
  numbers). It defaults to "Further" — no extra limit beyond the normal rippling reach — and
  members can drag it towards "Nearer" if they only want to see posts from close by. It's a
  personal preference, remembered between visits along with their other filters (which
  posts, which communities, and sort order).

### When they post, there's no group to choose
When a member creates a post, they **no longer pick which community it goes to**. This is
intentional. The post now simply reflects **where it actually is**, based on the postcode
they give, and rippling out then shows it to the **right people, closest first**, spreading
outwards over time.

So members no longer need to think about "which group should I post to?" or post to a
second group to get more eyes on an item - the system does that spreading for them
automatically. They just say what they're offering and where it is, and rippling handles
the rest.

### Occasionally, "you can't reply to this yet"
On their default **Nearby** view, a member only ever sees posts that have already reached
them, so they can always reply. Only if they move away from that default — switching to
"All my communities", say, or widening what they're looking at — might they occasionally
see a post that **hasn't yet rippled out to their area**. If so, they'll see a friendly
note instead of a reply box:

> *"We're showing this to people closest to it first — you'll be able to reply once it
> reaches your area."*

As soon as the post ripples out to where they are, the reply box appears and they can get
in touch as normal. **Nothing is lost** — it's just "not yet" rather than "no".

This applies however they reply — on the website, by email, or through TrashNothing. A
reply sent early is simply **held quietly** and delivered the moment the post reaches them.
(If the item is taken or withdrawn before it ever reaches them, they're told it's already
gone, so they're not left waiting.)

### Notifications follow the same rule
A member's **daily digest email** and **"new posts near you" notifications** only include a
post once it has rippled out to their area, so members are never nudged about something they
can't yet act on. The notification arrives when the post reaches them, at which point they can
reply straight away.

A nice side effect: because what they're shown is now driven by where they actually are and
spreads closest-first, the posts in their **"what's new" digest will feel more relevant** -
more genuinely local and worth a look, and less of the far-away clutter they'd have skimmed
past before.

### A short note in Help — and now also in the filters
There's an entry in **Help** called **"Which posts do I see?"** that explains all of this
in members' own terms. The same explanation now also opens as an in-place pop-up from the
**"How does this work?"** link in the browse filters, so members don't have to leave the
page to read it. It covers the filters themselves (which communities, which posts, sort
order, and the distance slider) as well as the reply caveat below. It's written as "how it
works" rather than "what's changed" — no new-change framing. The wording is:

> **Which posts do I see?**
> When someone offers something, we show it to people nearby first, then gradually ripple
> it out to people further away as time passes. This keeps freegling local — neighbours get
> first chance to collect, which means less travel and a fairer chance for everyone. The
> feed shows the most relevant posts first — nearest and newest, unseen ones first — and
> you can use the distance slider to see only posts from closer by.
>
> Normally you only see posts that have already reached your area, so you can always reply
> to them. If you change from the default view — for example widening the distance, or
> switching which posts you see — you might occasionally find a post that hasn't rippled
> out to your area yet. As soon as it reaches you, you'll be able to reply.

---

## What members will *not* see

- They **won't** see any of the moderation side — pending lists, held-reply reasons, or
  which community a post started on. To them it's simply "I can see this and reply" or
  "I'll be able to reply soon".
- Aside from no longer choosing a group when posting (above), they **won't** have to change
  how they post or reply. They offer and request much as before; they just give the location
  and let the system do the spreading.

---

## Why it might come up in member questions

The main thing a member might ask you about is **"why can't I reply to this post?"** The
answer is always the same and reassuring:

> *The post hasn't rippled out to your area yet. It's being shown to people closest to it
> first. As soon as it reaches you, you'll be able to reply — you don't need to do
> anything.*

That's the whole change from a member's point of view: **posts reach people gradually,
closest first**, and very occasionally that means waiting a little before they can reply.
