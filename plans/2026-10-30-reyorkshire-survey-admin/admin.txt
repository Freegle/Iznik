Do you know, or are you involved with, a community organisation in York and North Yorkshire? If so, we'd love your help.

The ReYorkshire Circular Economy & Community Survey is now live, and the ReYorkshire team want to hear from as many local organisations as possible.

WHAT IS REYORKSHIRE?

It's a collaborative project, run in partnership with North Yorkshire Council and City of York Council, that aims to increase reuse, reduce waste and support circular economy initiatives across the region. Over the next 18 months, the team will look at which waste streams could become valuable resources and set up pilot projects to reuse them across different sectors.

WHY THE SURVEY?

Our region is full of organisations doing great work to support others. The survey will help us understand:

- How interested and involved community organisations are in reuse and recycling
- The challenges they face
- Needs and opportunities linked to upcoming refurbishment and new-build projects

Your responses will give valuable insight to the ReYorkshire project and both councils. They will also help highlight the important work organisations are doing, which could open the door to extra funding to grow or diversify.

A BONUS FOR ORGANISATIONS WITH BUILDING PLANS

If an organisation has a refurbishment or building project coming up, ReYorkshire may be able to help source low-cost, low-carbon materials.
