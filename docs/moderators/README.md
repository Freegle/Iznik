# Moderating Freegle: a guide for volunteers

Moderators are the volunteers who help run Freegle. Moderation is national: there is no
local community to belong to or switch between. You use **ModTools** (modtools.org) to
look after posts, members, spam and reports, alongside the checks Freegle already runs
itself.

This guide is organised around what you actually do:

1. **[Getting started](getting-started.md)** - getting into ModTools, roles and
   permissions, and the home page.
2. **[Moderating posts](moderating-posts.md)** - published and taken-down posts, why a
   post was taken down, standard messages, and how reports and restores work.
3. **[Managing members](managing-members.md)** - new and flagged members, spammers, chat
   review, notes, and banning.

## First principles

A few things worth holding onto, whichever guide you are in:

- **Assume good faith.** Most members are kind and enthusiastic. Ban only with real
  evidence of harm, not because someone posts a lot.
- **Prefer editing over taking down.** If a post breaks a small rule, edit it with a
  friendly note rather than taking it down. Take one down only for the core rules: it
  must be **free** and **legal**.
- **Be personal, not corporate.** Members appreciate a friendly human reply, not a memo.
  Standard messages are a starting point, not a script.
- **ModTools never gates.** Freegle checks and publishes posts itself. You are adding a
  second opinion where it helps, not clearing a queue before anything can go live.
- If you are ever stuck, email the mentors at `mentors@ilovefreegle.org`, or use the
  volunteers' Discourse forum (the "Us" link in ModTools).

There is a member-facing guide to how far a post travels at
[../members/rippling-out.md](../members/rippling-out.md), and the technical detail is in
[../developers/reference/rippling-algorithm.md](../developers/reference/rippling-algorithm.md).

## Other ModTools pages

A few pages do not fit the home page's nine lists, but are still worth knowing about:

- **Standard message sets** (`/settings`) - the canned, editable replies covered in
  [Moderating posts](moderating-posts.md#standard-messages), configured nationally.
- **Stories and newsletters** (`/members/stories`, `/members/newsletter`) - member stories
  and what goes out in the newsletter.
- **Broadcasts** (`/admins`) - essential and newsletter messages sent to everyone.
- **Logs** (`/logs`) - the audit trail of moderator actions.
- **Rippling explorer** (`/rippling`) - visualises how far a given post's reach goes. See
  [how far a post travels](../members/rippling-out.md).
- **Gift Aid and Clearances** (`/giftaid`, `/helper-escalated`) - specialist tools, gated
  by their own permissions rather than by role.
- **Support and sysadmin tools** (`/support`, `/sysadmin`, `/images`) - gated by the
  Support and Admin system-wide roles.
