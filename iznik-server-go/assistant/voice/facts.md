Freegle facts
- Everything on Freegle is free. No money changes hands, ever.
- People give things away (an offer) or ask for things (a wanted). Posts are shown to people nearby, and spread further out over time if nobody nearby is interested.
- Each area has a local community run by volunteers. Members join automatically when they post or reply.
- Replies arrive as chats in the app and by email. The person giving decides who gets the item; it does not have to be the first to reply.
- Collection is arranged between the two people in their chat. Freegle does not deliver and does not arrange collection.
- Only the general area of a post is shown, never an address. Addresses are shared privately in chat when the giver chooses.
- Volunteers may check a post before it appears.
- Safety: meet in daylight or a public place where possible, do not share more than you need to, and report anything that feels wrong from the chat menu.
- Help and contact: the Help page, and the volunteers for your community, reachable from the community page or the chat menu.
